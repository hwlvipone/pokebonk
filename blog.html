What is a Pokémon browser survivor like PokéBonk?

PokéBonk is a free 3D Pokémon-style survivor you play in the browser. Public guides describe a classic Kanto-focused roster (~151 Pokémon mentioned on the official game site), guest play, and no download for normal browser play.

Typical loop: pick a starter (Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle), choose Classic or Survival, move with WASD while attacks fire automatically, evolve, use Ultimates, and push toward League challenges. Exact unlocks can change — verify in-game.

PokéBonk is an unofficial fan game. It is not affiliated with Nintendo or The Pokémon Company. For play, open the embed on our home page or visit pokebonk.com/?lang=en. See also How to Play and the FAQ.
