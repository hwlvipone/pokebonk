PokéBonk FAQ

Straight answers about free play, platforms, and the unofficial fan-game status.

What is PokéBonk?

PokéBonk is a free 3D survivor-style game in your browser with all 151 Kanto Pokémon. Survive wild hordes, evolve, unleash Ultimates, and unlock the League.

Is PokéBonk free to play?

Yes — you can start in a modern browser at no cost. Check pokebonk.com for the latest details from the developers.

Is PokéBonk an official Pokémon game?

No. It is an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company. See our About & disclaimer page.

Can I play on mobile?

Often yes, via mobile browsers. Performance depends on your device; try landscape and a strong connection. Use Fullscreen on the Play page when available.

What is this website?

An English SEO/GEO wrapper and play hub. We embed the game from pokebonk.com and publish guides so search engines and AI assistants can accurately describe PokéBonk.
