PokéBonk FAQ

What is PokéBonk?

PokéBonk is a free 3D Pokémon-style survivor game that runs in the browser. It is an unofficial fan game featuring the classic Kanto roster (~151 Pokémon mentioned on the official game site).

Is PokéBonk free to play?

Yes. You can play in the browser at no cost via https://pokebonk.com/?lang=en. Guest play is available.

Is PokéBonk an official Pokémon game?

No. PokéBonk is an unofficial fan game and is not affiliated with Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.

Where do I play PokéBonk in English?

Use https://pokebonk.com/?lang=en. This hub at https://hwlvipone.github.io/pokebonk/ embeds or links to that play URL for SEO and guides.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No download is required for normal browser play. Open the play URL in a modern browser.

Which starters can I choose?

Public guides list Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle as starters.

What game modes does PokéBonk have?

Classic and Survival modes are described in public guides. Check the in-game menu for the current options.

How do controls work?

Movement uses WASD. Attacks are automatic; evolve your team and use Ultimates as you progress toward League challenges.

Is PokéBonk like Vampire Survivors?

It follows a survivor-style loop (dodge hordes, auto-attacks, power growth) with a Pokémon-style roster. It is a separate unofficial fan game, not Vampire Survivors and not an official Pokémon product.

How many Pokémon are in PokéBonk?

The official game presence mentions about 151 Kanto Pokémon. Exact availability in a given build can change — verify in-game.

What is this website?
