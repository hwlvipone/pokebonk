How to Play PokéBonk

A quick guide to surviving Kanto hordes in this free 3D browser survivor — controls, progression, and tips.

1. Start a run

Open the Play page and use the embedded game (or visit pokebonk.com). Choose your starting Pokémon and dive in — no download required.

2. Move, dodge, collect

  • Desktop: typically WASD or arrow keys to move; mouse may aim or interact depending on build.
  • Gamepad: supported when the browser grants gamepad access (allowed on our embed).
  • Mobile: on-screen touch controls when available; landscape often feels better for longer runs.

Stay mobile. Hordes scale quickly — weaving between enemies while picking up XP and items is the core loop.

3. Evolve and power up

As you level, pick upgrades and evolve Pokémon. Stronger forms and synergies make late waves manageable. Watch cooldowns for special abilities.

4. Unleash your Ultimate

Ultimates turn fights when the screen fills up. Save them for dense packs or boss-like pressure rather than every small skirmish.

5. Unlock the League

Push successful runs to unlock League content and longer-term goals. Treat early deaths as learning — spacing and upgrade order matter more than raw aggression.

Tips for longer runs

Prioritize survival

One more second of positioning often beats greedy DPS picks when the map is crowded.

Learn patterns

Enemy spawn density and telegraphing get clearer after a few runs — use that knowledge.

Fullscreen helps

On the Play page, use Fullscreen for fewer distractions and a larger playfield.
