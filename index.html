What is PokéBonk?

PokéBonk is a free 3D Pokémon-style survivor you play in the browser. It is an unofficial fan game built around the classic Kanto roster (~151 Pokémon mentioned on the official game site).

Jump in as a guest, pick a starter (Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle), and choose Classic or Survival. Move with WASD, rely on auto-attacks, evolve your team, trigger Ultimates, and push toward League challenges.

How to Play PokéBonk

Open the game

Go to https://pokebonk.com/?lang=en (or use the Play embed on this hub). PokéBonk runs in modern browsers. Guest play lets you start without creating an account.

Pick a starter and mode

Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle. Select Classic or Survival depending on the run length and challenge you want.

Move, survive, auto-attack

Use WASD to dodge and reposition. Attacks fire automatically — focus on positioning, pickups, and surviving escalating hordes in this Vampire Survivors–style loop.

Evolve and Ultimate

Grow and evolve your Pokémon as you progress. Unleash Ultimates when available to clear pressure and push deeper into the run.

League challenges

Public guides describe League progression as a longer-term goal after you learn the core survivor loop. Exact unlock rules may change with game updates — check in-game prompts.
