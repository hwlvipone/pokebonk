What is PokéBonk?

PokéBonk is a free 3D Pokémon-style survivor you play in the browser. It is an unofficial fan game built around the classic Kanto roster (~151 Pokémon mentioned on the official game site).

Jump in as a guest, pick a starter (Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle), and choose Classic or Survival. Move with WASD, rely on auto-attacks, evolve your team, trigger Ultimates, and push toward League challenges.
