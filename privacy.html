Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22. This policy describes how the static PokéBonk play hub at playpokebonk.org handles information.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the English play hub hosted at https://playpokebonk.org/ (the “Hub”). The Hub is a static website (HTML/CSS/JS) that embeds or links to third-party services. The playable game is provided by pokebonk.com and is not operated by this Hub.

2. What this Hub collects

No first-party accounts. The Hub does not offer user registration or a first-party login.

First-party analytics. The Hub uses Google Analytics via Google tag (gtag.js) with measurement ID G-MXD7JQJ7YP. Google may collect device/browser technical data, approximate location, and page-view information according to Google’s policies. You can limit analytics via browser controls, Google’s opt-out tools, or cookie settings where available.

Server / CDN logs. Whatever host serves the Hub (for example GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) may automatically collect standard request logs such as IP address, user agent, referrer, and timestamps. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider under their policies—not by custom Hub application code.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Hub loads Google Analytics (gtag), which may use cookies or similar storage for measurement. Separately, embedded third parties may set cookies or use local storage when their content loads:

  • Game iframe — content from pokebonk.com may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for gameplay, language, guest/session state, or their own analytics. See their privacy materials if available.
  • YouTube embeds — videos loaded from youtube.com may set cookies and collect data according to Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your browser settings control whether third-party cookies are allowed. Blocking them may affect embeds.

4. Children

The Hub is a general audience fan site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through first-party Hub forms (there are none). If you believe a child has provided personal data to a third-party service linked here, contact that service directly.

5. International visitors

The Hub is published for a global English audience. Hosting and third-party providers may process technical data in various countries. We do not sell personal information collected by first-party Hub code (we do not collect such profiles).

6. Your choices

  • Use browser controls to block third-party cookies or clear site data.
  • Avoid loading embeds (for example, do not open pages with the game or YouTube iframes) if you do not want those third parties to run.
  • Review the privacy practices of pokebonk.com and YouTube/Google for their processing.

7. Contact

For privacy questions about this Hub, open an issue on the public GitHub repository hwlvipone/pokebonk or use any contact method later listed on the About page. For privacy matters about the game itself, contact the operators of pokebonk.com.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a new version on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
